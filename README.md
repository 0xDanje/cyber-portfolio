# cyber-portfolio

I was introduced to cybersecurity for my job in the military as a computer systems hardware repairer. At the time I thought little of it but my unit had all personell in those rolls study and take Security+. I was talented and interested so I used that certification to start my career in tech and ultimately in a role doing cybersecurity.

The NCL CTF was a fun challenge that really brought me back to what I love about cybersecurity, solving problems, disecting technology, and accomplishing things through understanding derived by trial and error.
The biggest highlight for me for this NCL CTF was achieving 5th place in the Cryptography category during the Individual game!
